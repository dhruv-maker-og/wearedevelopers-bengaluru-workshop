No confidential content

These workshop resources are public. Do not paste secrets, credentials, proprietary source, customer data, personal data, or other confidential material into a prompt, issue, or repository.

Workshop content may be deleted

Lab repositories, workspaces, and organization content can be removed after the workshop. Keep personal work elsewhere and do not treat these repositories as durable storage.

Token is time-limited

The join link contains an expiring workshop token. If access fails after the event, use the support route defined by the organizer instead of sharing or reusing the token.
